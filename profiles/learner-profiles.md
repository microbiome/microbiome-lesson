## Is this lesson for me?

These are some of the people we had in mind when we wrote this lesson.

**Aino** is a first-year PhD student in microbiology. She has collected stool
samples and received a table of species abundances from a sequencing facility.
She has recently finished the *Introduction to data analysis with R and
Bioconductor* lesson but has never analysed microbiome data. She
wants to know where to start and which analyses are standard in the field.

**Einari** is a medical researcher studying colorectal cancer. He reads many
microbiome papers and wants to understand what alpha diversity, beta diversity
and differential abundance actually mean, so that he can judge the results of
others and run a first analysis himself.

**Lumi** is a bioinformatician who has analysed RNA-seq data with Bioconductor.
She has been asked to help with a microbiome project. She knows R and
`SummarizedExperiment` well, but she needs to learn what is special about
microbiome data and which tools to use.
